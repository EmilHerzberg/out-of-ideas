# Archetype: vocab_context

The question must define a word IN CONTEXT through usage, not asking for a
dictionary definition.

## Length budget (mobile UI)
- Question: ≤150 chars (aim ≤120). Sentence-context stems naturally include
  a sample sentence — keep that sentence ≤60 chars and the context framing
  ≤40 chars. Drop unnecessary attribution ("In the sentence..." → just
  quote the sentence).
- Each answer: ≤45 chars. Vocab answers should be short paraphrases ("rude
  manipulator", "cleverly fallacious", "unctuous flatterer") not full
  definitions.

## default-repertoire SAT-vocab to AVOID (see default-repertoire.txt)
"sophistical", "unctuous", "baroque" — well-trodden in AI training data.
Use less-saturated targets like "phlegmatic", "sere", "pellucid",
"obsequious", "abstemious".

Examples of stems (illustrative — these specific words are saturated):
- "In the sentence 'Her arguments were sophistical,' what does 'sophistical' suggest?"
- "When a chef calls a dish 'unctuous,' they're describing what?"
- "An author writing in a 'baroque' style is writing in what way?"

Distractor rule: the highest-plausibility distractor should be a "false
friend" — a word the target word resembles or is often confused with. Other
distractors should be sibling-meanings in the same semantic neighborhood.
